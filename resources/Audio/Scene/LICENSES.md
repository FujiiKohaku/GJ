## clear_transition_pour.wav / game_over_transition_splat.wav

- Title: 40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX
- Creator: rubberduck
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/water-splash-slime-sfx.zip
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded: 2026-09-09
- clear_transition_pour.wav: First 1.1 seconds of loop_water_01.ogg, with
  60 ms fade-in and 140 ms fade-out, matched to the clear transition's rising water.
- game_over_transition_splat.wav: slime_01.ogg, slime_04.ogg, slime_07.ogg,
  and slime_03.ogg, trimmed of leading silence and arranged at 0, 0.35, 0.70,
  and 1.302 seconds. The final sound coincides with the large central splat.
  Relative peak levels: 0.50, 0.42, 0.45, 1.0. Short edge fades applied.
- Both files: Mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV, peak normalized to 0.88.
- Use: One-shot cues when the clear screen fill or game-over screen impacts begin.
